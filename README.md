## Understanding SMS Marketing

SMS marketing is the process of using text messages to communicate promotional information with customers.

Unlike general advertising, SMS campaigns can be directed toward specific customer groups based on available business data.

Common SMS marketing activities include:

* Product promotions
* Discount announcements
* Seasonal offers
* New product launches
* Event invitations
* Limited-time campaigns
* Customer re-engagement
* Business announcements

The campaign should always have a clear purpose before messages are prepared.

## Building a Customer Segment

Audience segmentation is an important part of promotional messaging.

Instead of sending every campaign to the entire customer database, businesses can create groups based on useful customer information.

Possible segments include:

| Segment            | Example Campaign           |
| ------------------ | -------------------------- |
| New Customers      | Welcome promotion          |
| Existing Customers | Special offer              |
| Repeat Buyers      | Loyalty campaign           |
| Inactive Customers | Re-engagement offer        |
| Product Interest   | Product-specific promotion |
| Event Customers    | Event announcement         |

Better segmentation can make campaign content more relevant to the people receiving it.

## Creating Effective Promotional Messages

A promotional SMS should communicate its main point quickly.

A simple message structure can be:

```text
Offer
  +
Key Benefit
  +
Call to Action
  +
Business Information
```

For example, a business may communicate a limited-time offer and provide customers with a simple way to learn more.

When writing promotional messages, consider:

* Keep the main offer clear
* Avoid unnecessary wording
* Use understandable language
* Mention important conditions when required
* Include a clear action
* Keep the message relevant to the audience

The exact message length and format can depend on the campaign requirements and SMS platform.

## Promotional SMS vs Transactional SMS

Promotional and transactional messages serve different purposes.

| Promotional SMS       | Transactional SMS        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Marketing-focused     | Event or action-focused  |
| Offers and promotions | OTPs and notifications   |
| Product announcements | Order updates            |
| Campaign-based        | Usually system-triggered |
| Customer engagement   | Customer information     |

Keeping these communication types separate can make SMS management easier for businesses.

## Responsible SMS Marketing

Businesses should maintain organized customer records and follow relevant rules regarding promotional communication and consent.

Important considerations include:

* Use appropriate customer lists

Responsible messaging can help maintain a better relationship between a business and its customers.
